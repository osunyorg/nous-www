Rooted in Resilience
We are a dynamic initiative focused on powering the future of human rights through political organizing, consensus building, fostering natural and strategic allyship and mobilizing new narratives.  We believe the origin of all initiatives is the soul (nous) and turning towards the soul and human self is an urgent political act for human rights to not only survive but thrive.   We recognize that the advancement of human rights can no longer rely primarily on moral authority or legal obligations. The future of human rights work must be understood as part of political strategy, economic decision-making, and security governance. This requires new models of leadership, investment, and collective action rooted in popular power.   We believe futures methodologies will play a key role in this shift-not only in anticipating change, but in enabling actors to collectively explore alternatives, test assumptions, and align around actionable directions under conditions of uncertainty. We are graciously hosted by the Colmena Fund.

Un-Disciplining the Human Rights Discourse and Practice
The discipline of human rights, from its legalisms, professionalisation and NGOisation, to its tethering to state recognition and institutional validation, has produced a form of practice that is often more legible to power than disruptive of it. We approach un-disciplining as a deliberate loosening: a deliberate loosening of the boundaries between what counts as knowledge and who counts as a knowledge-holder, of the hierarchy that positions Global North institutions as the arbiters of universal claims, and of the de-politization of human rights. Un-disciplining invites us to draw from feminist organising, Indigenous lifeways, abolitionist futures, and care economies as equally valid frameworks for articulating and advancing human dignity. Un-disciplining is, ultimately, about returning human rights to the movements and communities that have always been its true authors, to re-wilding human rights as a living practice forged in struggle.
