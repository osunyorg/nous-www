Human Dignity in International Law. Cambridge University Press, 2021. summary: >-

The book traces how human dignity developed into a central principle of international law, examining the historical and philosophical ideas that shaped its recognition. Drawing on legal documents and international judicial decisions, it explores how dignity is understood and applied across international human rights law, humanitarian law, and international criminal law. It offers a historical and legal account of how the concept has evolved within the international legal system.
