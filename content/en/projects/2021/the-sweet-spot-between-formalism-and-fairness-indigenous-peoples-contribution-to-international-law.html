Indigenous Peoples are contributing to changes in international law by challenging its traditional focus on states and demanding greater recognition of their rights and authority. Charters examines their growing participation in international decision-making and the development of different forms of self-determination. She argues that international law can become more inclusive while retaining binding rules that protect communities against abuses of state power.
