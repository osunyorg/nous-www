Lithipokoroda. 2021. summary: >-

The film uses Indigenous performance and visual storytelling to confront colonial violence, environmental destruction, and the marginalisation of ancestral knowledge. Centred on an ancestral woman's journey through the forest, it explores the relationship between territory, cultural survival, and resistance. By bringing Indigenous voices and creative expression to the foreground, Lithipokoroda presents filmmaking as a way to reclaim narratives and assert cultural self-determination.
