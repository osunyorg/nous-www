Original Nation Approaches to Inter-National Law: The Quest for the Rights of Indigenous Peoples and Nature in the Age of Anthropocene. Cham: Palgrave Macmillan, 2021. summary: >-

The book challenges international law's traditional focus on states by examining the histories and struggles of Indigenous nations. Fukurai and Krooth argue that existing political and legal systems have contributed to the loss of Indigenous lands, cultural diversity and environmental protection. They propose an alternative approach that places Indigenous self-determination, cooperation between nations and the rights of nature at the centre of a more inclusive international legal order.
