Truth and Post-Truth in Public Policy: Interpreting the Arguments. Elements in Public Policy. Cambridge University Press, 2021. summary: >-

The book examines why providing more factual information is often insufficient to counter post-truth politics. Fischer argues that understanding misinformation and political disagreement also requires attention to how people interpret facts through social meanings, emotions, and political beliefs. Using debates about climate change and COVID-19, he develops an interpretive approach to policy analysis that examines how competing narratives shape public understanding and responses to scientific evidence.
