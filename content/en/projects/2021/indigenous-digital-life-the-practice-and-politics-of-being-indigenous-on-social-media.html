Indigenous Digital Life: The Practice and Politics of Being Indigenous on Social Media. Palgrave Macmillan, 2021. summary: >-

Drawing on several years of research into Indigenous Australians' experiences of social media, Carlson and Frazer challenge colonial representations of Indigenous peoples as belonging to the past. They examine how digital platforms shape identity, community, relationships, and experiences of discrimination, while also creating opportunities for Indigenous agency and creativity. The book presents Indigenous digital life as a future-oriented practice through which communities actively build connections, assert sovereignty, and imagine different social worlds.
