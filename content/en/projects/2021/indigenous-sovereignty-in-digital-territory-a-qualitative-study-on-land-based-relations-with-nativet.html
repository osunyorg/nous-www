The article examines how Indigenous language networks on Twitter sustain relationships with territories, cultures, and communities. The qualitative study identifies digital practices that connect people to homelands, support language revitalisation, and navigate settler colonialism online. It presents Indigenous digital sovereignty as extending beyond technological access to encompass land-based relationships, cultural continuity, and struggles for justice in digital environments.
