Gaete-Silva and Gaete examine changing attitudes towards disruptive behaviour, punishment and authority in contemporary societies. They argue that traditional disciplinary responses have lost social legitimacy and may worsen the problems they seek to address. The article connects these changes to evolving understandings of justice, individual rights and social participation, and considers alternative ways of responding to disruptive behaviour.
