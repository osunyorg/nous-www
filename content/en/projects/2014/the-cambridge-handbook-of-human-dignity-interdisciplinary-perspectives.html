The Cambridge Handbook of Human Dignity: Interdisciplinary Perspectives. Cambridge University Press, 2014. summary: >-

This interdisciplinary collection explores how human dignity has been understood across different historical periods, cultures, religious traditions, and philosophical approaches. It examines both European and non-Western perspectives, considers the relationship between dignity and human rights, and investigates how dignity is interpreted in law, justice, medicine, and other fields. The volume provides multiple perspectives for understanding the foundations, meanings, and practical applications of human dignity.
