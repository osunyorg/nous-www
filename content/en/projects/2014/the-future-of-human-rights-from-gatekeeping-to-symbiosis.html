Rodríguez-Garavito argues that the human rights movement needs to become more open, diverse, and collaborative. Rather than allowing large organisations and legal experts to dominate decisions, he proposes a system where grassroots groups, local organisations, activists, and international institutions work together as equal partners. This approach would help the movement respond to changing global power relations, new technologies, and environmental challenges while making better use of different forms of knowledge and experience.
