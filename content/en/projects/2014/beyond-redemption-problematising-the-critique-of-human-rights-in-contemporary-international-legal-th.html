Golder examines the human rights critiques developed by David Kennedy, Anne Orford and Makau Mutua, arguing that each ultimately returns to human rights as a framework with transformative possibilities. The article questions this tendency to combine criticism with renewed commitment, inviting reflection on the possibilities and limitations of reimagining human rights from within.
