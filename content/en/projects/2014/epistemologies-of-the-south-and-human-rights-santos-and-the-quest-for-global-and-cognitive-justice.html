Barreto examines Boaventura de Sousa Santos's ideas of abyssal thinking and Epistemologies of the South to reconsider the foundations of human rights. He challenges the exclusion of non-Western knowledge and experiences from dominant rights frameworks, exploring how colonial histories and intercultural dialogue can inform a more inclusive, post-abyssal understanding of human rights.
