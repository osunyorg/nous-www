Féron, Henri. “Human Rights and Faith: A ‘World-Wide Secular Religion’?” Ethics & Global Politics 7, no. 4 (2014): 181–200. summary: >-

Féron examines the similarities between human rights and religious belief, arguing that both depend on moral convictions that cannot be conclusively proven. He discusses how this creates tensions between human rights and other cultural or religious values. The article warns that imposing human rights through coercion can undermine their own principles and emphasises the importance of tolerance and mutual respect.
