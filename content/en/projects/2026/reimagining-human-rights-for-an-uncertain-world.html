Open Society Foundations. summary: >-

Nowrojee calls for reimagining human rights beyond defending existing institutions and responding to violations. She argues that human rights must be understood as a universal but plural project, shaped by different traditions and collective struggles rather than dominated by powerful states. Emphasising the limits of legal approaches alone, she advocates renewed political organising, broader coalitions, community leadership, and positive visions of justice that address inequality, technological power, and global uncertainty.
