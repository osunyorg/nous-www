The Past, Present and Future of the Human Rights Movement. Online panel discussion moderated by James A. Goldston, 8 October 2026. summary: >-

This discussion reflects on the human rights movement's historical achievements, institutional limitations and future challenges. Panellists examine the influence of colonialism, geopolitical power and grassroots struggles on human rights advocacy. They debate how the movement can respond to contemporary crises, address structural inequalities and strengthen its independence, political relevance and connection with affected communities.
