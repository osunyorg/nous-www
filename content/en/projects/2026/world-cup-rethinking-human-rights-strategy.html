Drawing on human rights campaigning around the Qatar World Cup, this conversation questions whether international criticism and public shaming effectively change attitudes or improve conditions for affected communities. Coombes and Bhutta explore alternative strategies based on locally relevant values, storytelling, intercultural dialogue, and opportunities for meaningful action. They argue that human rights organisations should combine accountability with approaches that build trust, recognise progress, and engage communities as participants in change.
