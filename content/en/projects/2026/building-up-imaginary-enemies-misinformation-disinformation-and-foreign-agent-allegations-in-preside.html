The report investigates how coordinated online disinformation campaigns in Indonesia have targeted journalists, activists, and civil society organisations by portraying them as agents of foreign interests. Amnesty International examines the involvement of state-linked actors, the relationship between online attacks and offline intimidation, and the role of social media platforms in amplifying harmful narratives. It highlights how disinformation can undermine freedom of expression, discourage civic participation, and increase risks faced by human rights defenders.
