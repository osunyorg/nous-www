Building a Free Internet of the Future. APC, 2 June 2026. summary: >-

This collection brings together interviews with people developing free and open digital technologies as alternatives to corporate-controlled platforms. Drawing on initiatives supported by NGI Zero, it highlights practical approaches to accessibility, privacy, information sharing and community participation. The publication shows how technological choices reflect social and political values and how communities can help shape more inclusive digital futures.
