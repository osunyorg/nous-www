Disaster Displacement and International Protection in the EU: Challenges, Misconceptions, and the Way Forward. London: Routledge, 2026. summary: >-

Scissa examines why EU asylum systems struggle to protect people displaced across borders by disasters. Drawing on legislation, policies and case law, particularly from Italy, she identifies problems in how disaster-related harm is understood, how protection criteria are applied and how restrictive asylum policies limit access to protection. The book advocates more flexible interpretations of existing legal frameworks.
