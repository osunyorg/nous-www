As commercial space activities expand, Barentine raises concerns about the exclusion of Indigenous communities whose cultural and spiritual traditions give the Moon special significance. Using disputes over sending human remains to the lunar surface, he questions whether existing space laws adequately respect different cultural perspectives. He calls for Indigenous peoples to help shape future decisions about space, rather than simply being consulted after plans are made.
