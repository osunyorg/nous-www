Chișinău Declaration. CM(2026)99-final, adopted 15 May 2026. summary: >-

Adopted by the Council of Europe's 46 member states, the Chișinău Declaration addresses how the European Convention on Human Rights should operate in the context of contemporary migration challenges. It reaffirms states' commitment to the Convention and the independence of the European Court of Human Rights, while emphasising national responsibilities for border control and public safety. The declaration addresses protections against ill-treatment, private and family life, and proposals for managing migration within the Convention framework.
