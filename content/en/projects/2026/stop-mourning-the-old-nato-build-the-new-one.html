Dalay argues that European security cannot rely indefinitely on NATO's traditional dependence on American leadership. Rather than attempting to restore the previous transatlantic order, he proposes a more adaptable European security architecture that includes non-EU NATO members, engages neighbouring regions, and uses flexible coalitions to address specific challenges. The article invites broader reflection on how established international institutions might adapt when geopolitical relationships and assumptions about global power change.
