Religious beliefs and gender equality are not necessarily in conflict. Petersen and Borchgrevink examine how feminist theologians, LGBTQ+ activists and faith-based organisations challenge discriminatory religious interpretations and promote gender justice. Through examples from Africa, Asia and Latin America, they describe how activists reinterpret religious teachings, create more inclusive communities and connect human rights advocacy with religious traditions, often while facing opposition from both religious conservatives and secular movements.
