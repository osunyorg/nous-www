Drawing on discussions convened by the International Network of Civil Liberties Organizations, Santos examines the need to rethink human rights strategies amid political, institutional, and financial pressures. She highlights proposals for strengthening grassroots organising, reducing dependence on international donors, engaging audiences beyond established activist circles, and recognising the political dimensions of human rights advocacy. The article also emphasises creating dedicated space for collective reflection and long-term planning rather than remaining focused exclusively on immediate crises.
