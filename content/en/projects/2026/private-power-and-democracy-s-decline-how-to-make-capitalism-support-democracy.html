The book examines how technological innovation and concentrated corporate power can contribute to economic inequality and undermine democracy. Focusing on the United States, Kurz argues that unregulated capitalism allows dominant firms to accumulate lasting market power while exposing workers to economic insecurity. He proposes a more regulated economic system, including policies to protect livelihoods, so that technological progress benefits society more broadly without reinforcing political and economic inequalities.
