Ayalew, Bello y Villarino and Johns examine the first UN Global Dialogue on AI Governance and its potential to support more inclusive international cooperation. They highlight disagreements over human rights protections, unequal access to AI technologies and the influence of powerful states and corporations. The authors question whether the Dialogue can move beyond discussion to strengthen accountability and participation, particularly for developing countries and marginalised communities.
