In this interview, former South African Foreign Minister Naledi Pandor reflects on the unequal distribution of power in international politics and the need for stronger international solidarity. Drawing on South Africa's anti-apartheid struggle and its legal action concerning Gaza, she discusses the limitations of existing international institutions, the effects of Islamophobia, and the importance of principled political leadership. She also emphasises organised collective action, diplomatic cooperation, and greater participation by Global South countries in shaping international affairs.
