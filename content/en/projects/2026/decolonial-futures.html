Decolonial Futures [Podcast series]. University of Melbourne, 2026. summary: >-

The podcast brings together researchers, artists, and creative practitioners to explore how colonial histories continue to shape contemporary societies and how alternative futures might be imagined. Through conversations connecting scholarship and creative practice, it examines approaches to challenging colonial power, rethinking dominant narratives, and creating space for different perspectives and ways of understanding the world.
