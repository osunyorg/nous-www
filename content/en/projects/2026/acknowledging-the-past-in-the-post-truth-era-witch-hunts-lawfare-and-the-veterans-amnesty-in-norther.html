The article examines how the British government used narratives of “witch-hunts” and “lawfare” to justify an amnesty for military veterans who served in Northern Ireland. Through this case study, McEvoy explores how post-truth political discourse can undermine efforts to investigate historical wrongdoing, challenging established assumptions about the relationship between law, truth, and accountability in transitional justice.
