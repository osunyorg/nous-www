Moussa and Samson critically examine the use of self-defence arguments in the context of Gaza, situating the violence following 7 October 2023 within a longer history of occupation and dispossession. They question how international legal doctrines can legitimise continued violence and argue that existing legal and political frameworks have failed to prevent mass atrocities and protect Palestinian rights.
