Open Society Foundations. summary: >-

Nowrojee argues that the future of international human rights is increasingly being shaped by grassroots movements, younger generations, legal advocates, and states outside traditional centres of global power. Challenging narratives of inevitable decline, she highlights how these actors combine political mobilisation with legal strategies and advocate broader understandings of rights, including economic, collective, and environmental justice. She calls for international institutions and philanthropic organisations to share power and support community-led efforts.
