Halder examines how responsibility for climate-related harm could be allocated among states following the ICJ's 2025 Climate Advisory Opinion. Drawing on principles of state responsibility, earlier judicial opinions and climate attribution science, he proposes a two-stage approach to assessing collective contributions and individual liability. The article considers the challenges of translating historical emissions into legally justified claims for reparations.
