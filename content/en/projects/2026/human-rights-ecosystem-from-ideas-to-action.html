Human Rights Ecosystem: From Ideas to Action. 2026. summary: >-

This strategy note calls for transforming a fragmented human rights sector by shifting power toward affected communities and strengthening collaboration between organisations. It questions approaches that prioritise collecting evidence for distant international mechanisms without ensuring meaningful redress. Videre proposes four practical steps: assessing AI's implications for human rights defenders, establishing shared support services, improving coordination around evidence, and developing stronger rapid-response systems to support locally led accountability.

It questions approaches that prioritise collecting evidence for distant international mechanisms without ensuring meaningful redress. Videre proposes four practical steps: assessing AI's implications for human rights defenders, establishing shared support services, improving coordination around evidence, and developing stronger rapid-response systems to support locally led accountability.
