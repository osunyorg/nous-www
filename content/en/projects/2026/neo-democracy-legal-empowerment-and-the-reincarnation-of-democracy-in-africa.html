Atuguba challenges the idea that the difficulties facing Western liberal democracy necessarily signal democracy's decline everywhere. Drawing on African constitutional traditions and grassroots legal practices, he proposes a model of democracy centred on legal empowerment: people's ability to understand, use, and shape the law. He argues that democratic renewal requires more than elections and formal institutions, emphasising accessible justice, social welfare, citizen participation, and locally grounded approaches to governance.
