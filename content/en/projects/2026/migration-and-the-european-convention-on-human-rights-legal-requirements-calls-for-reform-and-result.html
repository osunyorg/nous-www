Migration and the European Convention on Human Rights: Legal Requirements, Calls for Reform and Resulting Developments. European Parliamentary Research Service, 2026. summary: >-

This briefing examines growing political pressure to reconsider the application of the European Convention on Human Rights to migration and deportation. It explains the protections provided by Articles 3 and 8, reviews calls for reform, and analyses the Council of Europe's 2026 Chișinău Declaration. It explores tensions between migration-control priorities, individual rights, and judicial independence, while considering how political developments may influence the future interpretation of established human rights protections.
