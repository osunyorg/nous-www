Salamanca-Aguado examines the first provisional measures orders concerning the International Seabed Authority's supervision of deep-sea mining contractors. The article explains how the Seabed Disputes Chamber balanced the Authority's regulatory powers with contractors' procedural rights, while interpreting both within the common heritage of humankind framework. It highlights the importance of institutional accountability and procedural fairness in governing seabed resources.
