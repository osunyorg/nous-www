The article examines how the legacies of slavery and colonialism continue to affect health, development, and environmental conditions in the Caribbean, increasing the region's vulnerability to climate change. It presents the Caribbean Community Reparations Commission's Ten-Point Action Plan as a framework for addressing historical injustices through measures such as debt cancellation, public health investment, and cultural preservation, while also responding to present inequalities and future environmental challenges.
