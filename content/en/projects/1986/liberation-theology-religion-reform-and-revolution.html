Liberation Theology: Religion, Reform, and Revolution: A Research Paper. GI 86-10028. Washington, DC: CIA, April 1986. summary: >-

This CIA research paper examines liberation theology as a force for political and social change, particularly in Latin America. It considers how Christian grassroots organising, demands for socioeconomic reform and relationships with revolutionary movements could influence existing political structures. Written from a US intelligence perspective, the report reveals how liberation theology's mobilisation of marginalised communities was interpreted within Cold War security debates.
