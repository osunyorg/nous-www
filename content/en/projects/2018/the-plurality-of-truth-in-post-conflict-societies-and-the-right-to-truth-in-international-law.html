Wijenayake examines how understandings of truth differ within post-conflict societies and how these differences affect transitional justice. Tracing the right to truth through international humanitarian and human rights law, she argues that legal approaches should recognise multiple dimensions of truth rather than assume a single, uniform understanding. The article explores how the right might respond to the particular needs of different post-conflict contexts.
