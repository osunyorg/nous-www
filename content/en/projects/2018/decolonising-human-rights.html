Amnesty International. summary: >-

The speech argues that decolonising human rights requires reconnecting the international human rights movement with ordinary people's struggles against abuses of power. Shetty examines how governments, powerful states, and international organisations have used or controlled human rights for their own interests, often weakening the influence of affected communities. He calls for greater grassroots leadership, stronger connections between political and socioeconomic rights, and renewed attention to emerging challenges posed by corporate power and technology.
