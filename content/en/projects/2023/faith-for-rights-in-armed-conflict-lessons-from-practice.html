Salama and Wiener examine how the Faith for Rights framework has been applied in situations of armed conflict. Through examples from Afghanistan, Cyprus and the Democratic Republic of the Congo, they discuss the role of religious actors in addressing human rights challenges and promoting dialogue. The article also explores how practical exercises and shared learning can support conflict mediation, cooperation and the prevention of incitement to hatred.
