This symposium examines the implications of UN recognition of the right to a clean, healthy and sustainable environment. Its contributors consider how the right may influence international and regional law, environmental accountability and the development of new legal standards. The essays also explore its limitations, particularly in relation to economic inequality, environmental destruction, the rights of nature and the protection of future generations.
