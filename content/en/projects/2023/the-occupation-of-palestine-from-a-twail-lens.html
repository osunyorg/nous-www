Brill, 2023. summary: >-

Murphy, Ferrara and Power apply TWAIL to examine how international law operates in the context of Israel's prolonged occupation of Palestine. They critique the use of legal arguments to justify military practices and examine how Israeli judicial decisions may reinforce interpretations that favour military authority. The chapter considers how international legal institutions and scholarship can contribute to maintaining unequal power relations.
