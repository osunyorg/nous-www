Faith-based organisations can contribute to human rights protection through community advocacy, humanitarian assistance and cooperation with governments and civil society. In this speech, Frank Schwabe highlights their work on poverty, religious freedom and environmental justice, including a case involving Indigenous communities affected by mining in Guatemala. He also discusses the importance of protecting religious human rights defenders and involving faith communities in international development partnerships.
