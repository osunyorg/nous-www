FORGE 2023. Video collection from the Future of Rights and Governance inaugural conference, New York, 3–5 November 2023. summary: >-

This collection brings together presentations exploring the future of human rights and global governance. Speakers address Indigenous knowledge, ecological justice, democracy, political imagination and institutional transformation. Through academic discussions, activist perspectives and artistic performances, the videos consider how human rights thinking and practice might evolve in response to contemporary crises and emerging challenges.
