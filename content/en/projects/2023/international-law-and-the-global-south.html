This episode discusses how international law has been shaped by European colonialism and unequal global power relations. Professor Obiora Chinedu Okafor considers the criticism that international legal institutions have contributed to injustice and exploitation, and the importance of examining international law from Global South perspectives. The conversation raises questions about how international law might better address inequality and recognise perspectives historically marginalised in its development.
