Gonzalez examines the Global South's role in developing the right to a healthy environment and how regional human rights courts have interpreted it. She considers whether these legal developments can address limitations of traditional human rights approaches, including their emphasis on individuals over communities, their human-centred understanding of nature, and their difficulties in responding to environmental harm across borders and economic structures.
