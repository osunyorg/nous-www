Stackhouse examines the historical and theological connections between Christianity and modern human rights. He argues that Christian ideas about human dignity, moral responsibility and individual freedom have contributed to the development of universal rights. He also discusses how religious traditions and human rights principles can support pluralism, while questioning whether human rights can maintain their authority without a deeper moral or religious foundation.
