Ferstman outlines the legal principles and purposes of reparations for internationally wrongful acts. She explains different forms of redress, including restitution, compensation, rehabilitation and satisfaction, alongside guarantees of non-repetition. The article highlights how reparations can address past harm, restore dignity and relationships, and contribute to preventing future violations through institutional and legal reform.
