Rajagopal examines how international law has contributed to global inequalities and distinguishes between powerful Global South states and grassroots movements challenging existing structures. He argues that the growing influence of emerging economies does not necessarily lead to a fairer international order. The essay also questions prevailing models of economic development and considers how social movements could contribute to more democratic international governance.
