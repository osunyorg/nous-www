Examining Türkiye's main opposition party between 2002 and 2022, Korkmaz explores how opposition strategies can influence democratic backsliding. She distinguishes between rigid opposition, characterised by identity-based polarisation, and flexible opposition, involving broader alliances and more inclusive political language. The article argues that although flexibility can help opposition parties respond to growing authoritarianism, it also carries risks, including weakening party identity and potentially reinforcing the political dynamics it seeks to challenge.
