Choi-Fitzpatrick examines how changes in geopolitics, climate, energy, science and technology could transform the future of human rights. He suggests that these developments may mark the beginning of a new era, rather than simply another stage in the evolution of existing rights. The article explores ways to understand such transformations and proposes hypotheses and practical recommendations for anticipating possible changes.
