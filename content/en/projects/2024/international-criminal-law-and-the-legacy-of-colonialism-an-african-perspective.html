International Criminal Law and the Legacy of Colonialism: An African Perspective. In cooperation with Luca Hauffe. Berlin: Humboldt-Universität zu Berlin, 2024. summary: >-

This edited volume brings together predominantly African scholars to examine the relationship between international criminal justice and colonialism. It considers whether contemporary institutions reproduce colonial power relations, how African approaches to accountability might inform alternative models of justice, and whether international criminal law can address historical colonial crimes. The contributions also explore the roles of African judges and perspectives in shaping international criminal justice.
