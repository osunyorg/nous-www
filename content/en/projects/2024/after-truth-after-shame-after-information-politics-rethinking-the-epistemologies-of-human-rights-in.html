Rethinking the Epistemologies of Human Rights in the Digital-Authoritarian Conjuncture.” The International Journal of Human Rights 28, nos. 8–9 (2024): 1501–1525. summary: >-

Bowsher examines how post-truth politics and reactionary digital cultures challenge human rights advocacy strategies based on documenting abuses and publicly shaming governments. Drawing on Donna Haraway and Maurizio Lazzarato, he proposes a more situated and politically engaged approach to producing human rights knowledge. The article considers how advocacy might move beyond reliance on factual exposure alone.
