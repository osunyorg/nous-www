Bakish Rao: Plantas en Lucha. 2024. summary: >-

Created by Indigenous artists from Brazil and Peru, this experimental science-fiction film imagines ecological destruction and resistance from the perspective of plants. Challenging human-centred narratives, it explores the consequences of monoculture and the homogenisation of living environments. Through speculative storytelling, the film invites audiences to consider relationships between human and nonhuman life, offering an alternative way to imagine ecological futures beyond conventional ideas of development and exploitation.
