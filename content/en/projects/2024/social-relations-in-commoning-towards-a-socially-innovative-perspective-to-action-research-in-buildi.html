The chapter examines how social relationships and individual and collective behaviour influence the creation, development, and decline of shared land resources. It highlights the importance of freedom and solidarity in building cooperative relationships and developing forms of governance that can resist unequal power structures. It also considers how these insights can inform action research on commons and their governance.
