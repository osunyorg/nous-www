TWAIL Reader (Edward Elgar, 2024). summary: >-

International law is often taught without sufficient attention to its colonial history and the inequalities it has helped sustain. Al Attar and Quintero argue that teaching informed by Third World Approaches to International Law (TWAIL) can provide a more critical understanding of the field. They suggest that recognising international law's historical role in maintaining unequal power relations is necessary for imagining a fairer international legal system.
