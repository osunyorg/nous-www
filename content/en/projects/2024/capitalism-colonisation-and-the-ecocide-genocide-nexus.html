Capitalism, Colonisation and the Ecocide-Genocide Nexus. University of London Press, 2024. summary: >-

Crook examines the relationship between colonialism, capitalism and ecological destruction, focusing on Indigenous and place-based communities in Kenya and Australia. Combining political-economic analysis with original interviews, he explores how land dispossession, extractive industries and development policies can threaten communities' cultural and physical survival. The book connects historical colonial violence with contemporary environmental injustice.
