In the Long Run: The Future as a Political Idea. London: Profile Books, 2024. summary: >-

White traces how changing ideas about the future have influenced political thought and democratic life. He considers how climate change, war and other crises challenge assumptions that tomorrow will bring opportunities for improvement. The book argues that maintaining a commitment to long-term political possibilities is important for democracy when uncertainty and the prospect of irreversible harm increasingly shape public life.
