Cunningham and Samson trace continuities between historical education programmes for working-class children and contemporary widening participation policies in English higher education. They argue that initiatives presented as expanding opportunity can also reproduce social hierarchies by preparing disadvantaged students for existing labour structures. Their analysis questions meritocratic narratives and highlights the growing financial burden placed on students seeking social mobility.
