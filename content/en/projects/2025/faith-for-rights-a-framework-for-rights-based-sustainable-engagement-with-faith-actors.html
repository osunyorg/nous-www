Salama and Wiener examine how the Faith for Rights framework connects religious communities with international human rights principles. They discuss the Beirut Declaration, its 18 commitments and the #Faith4Rights toolkit, which encourages practical cooperation and learning among participants. The article describes how these resources have been used by different institutions and proposes strengthening networks for continued dialogue, knowledge-sharing and joint action.
