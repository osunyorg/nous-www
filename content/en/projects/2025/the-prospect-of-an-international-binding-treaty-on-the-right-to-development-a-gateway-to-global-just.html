Tadeg examines the proposed International Covenant on the Right to Development as a framework for addressing global inequalities. He argues that discussions should move beyond recognising the right towards its practical implementation, particularly in relation to international trade, intellectual property, debt, climate finance and development financing. The article considers how binding international commitments could strengthen the realisation of the right to development.
