Afogo examines how international judicial decisions contributed to the 2025 UK–Mauritius agreement concerning the Chagos Archipelago. Drawing on rulings from the ICJ, maritime arbitration and ITLOS, he explores their influence on the recognition of Mauritian sovereignty and the right to self-determination. The blog post also considers the agreement's provisions on reparations and the possibilities for Chagossian resettlement.
