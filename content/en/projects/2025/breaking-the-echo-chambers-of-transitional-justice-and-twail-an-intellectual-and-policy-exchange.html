Aboueldahab examines the limitations of transitional justice and TWAIL, arguing that both fields have become constrained by insular debates and unrepresentative knowledge production. She proposes greater exchange between TWAIL's critical approaches to international law and transitional justice's practical orientation towards institutional and policy change. The article explores how this dialogue could help develop more inclusive understandings of justice and resistance.
