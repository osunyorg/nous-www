The authors examine tensions between the independence and accountability of UN human rights experts, particularly in relation to gender equality and gender-based violence. They argue that some recent interpretations risk weakening existing protections for transgender people and sex workers. The article calls for greater transparency, stronger professional standards, and constructive civil society oversight to prevent the erosion of rights while preserving expert independence.
