Decolonizing Development: Reparatory Justice for People and Planet. Expert commentary for the UNDP Development at Risk Report. United Nations Development Programme, 2025. summary: >-

Khoday links contemporary environmental vulnerability in the Caribbean to the enduring effects of colonial extraction and inequality. He argues that addressing these risks requires more than financial reparations or conventional development policies. Drawing on decolonial and ecological perspectives, the paper proposes rethinking development around the interdependence of communities and ecosystems, recognising both human and non-human agency.
