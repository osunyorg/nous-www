Pandey examines the legal recognition of Kashmiri Pandits through TWAIL, focusing on Indigenous rights and internal displacement. She argues that state narratives and geopolitical considerations have contributed to the community's exclusion from international protection frameworks. Drawing on legal analysis and displacement-related evidence, the article calls for greater attention to epistemic justice and the role of narratives in determining whose experiences receive legal recognition.
