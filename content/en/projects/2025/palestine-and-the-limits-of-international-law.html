Krever examines the limitations of international law in addressing violence and injustice in Palestine. He argues that focusing on the prosecution of individual political leaders can overlook the broader structures that enable violence. The article also considers proposals for a Gaza Tribunal and the history of people's tribunals, questioning whether legal approaches to accountability can support broader political struggles or risk narrowing their aims.
