The commentary explores how cinema can become a form of creative self-determination, enabling communities to represent their experiences, knowledge, and relationships with land on their own terms. Through two films by Indigenous creators, it examines how storytelling can challenge colonial narratives, communicate ecological perspectives beyond human-centred approaches, and encourage audiences to reconsider their relationship with nature and Indigenous claims to territorial sovereignty.
