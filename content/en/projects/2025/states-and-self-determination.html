Shahabuddin challenges the assumption that European models of statehood can be applied universally. Using Third World Approaches to International Law (TWAIL), he examines how colonial boundaries and political ideas influenced the formation of postcolonial states. The chapter argues that these processes have restricted self-determination for minority communities and highlights the importance of examining inequalities within postcolonial states, not only between states.
