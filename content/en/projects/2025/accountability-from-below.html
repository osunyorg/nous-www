Hunt and Kapilashrami question the dominance of state-centred human rights accountability and examine how communities and social movements can hold powerful actors accountable. Drawing on experiences from different regions, they propose a people-centred approach that combines grassroots organising with human rights standards. The editorial also outlines a possible independent global health accountability panel led by communities and civil society.
