McMahon, Kate. “Can the Global South Salvage International Law?” The New Humanitarian, 9 December 2025. summary: >-

The article examines how Global South countries are responding to concerns about the unequal enforcement of international law, particularly in relation to Palestine. McMahon focuses on the Hague Group, comparing its efforts with earlier movements for cooperation among formerly colonised countries. She discusses the political and economic obstacles these countries face and questions whether collective action can make international legal institutions more accountable and effective.
