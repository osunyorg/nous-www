McGovern and Fusco examine how epistemic disobedience, a concept developed by Walter Mignolo, can inform decolonial approaches to public health research and practice. They challenge colonial hierarchies in knowledge production and consider alternative approaches to research that recognise different experiences and perspectives. The article connects these debates to efforts to advance health equity as a human right.
