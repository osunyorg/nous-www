Q&A Human Rights Watch. “Why Reparations? Q&A.” 19 June 2025. summary: >-

Focusing on the United States, Human Rights Watch explains how slavery and subsequent racial discrimination continue to affect Black communities. It discusses different forms of repair, including financial payments, public acknowledgment, community investment, and institutional reform. The Q&A also outlines existing reparations initiatives and argues that meaningful solutions should be developed with the communities affected by historical and ongoing injustice.
