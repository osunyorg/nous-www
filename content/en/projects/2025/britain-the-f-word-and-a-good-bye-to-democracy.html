This episode features Luke de Noronha in a discussion about the growing influence of far-right politics in Britain and its implications for democracy, migration, and public debate. It examines how exclusionary political narratives can become increasingly accepted and considers the challenges facing those seeking to resist them. The conversation also raises questions about the political consequences of pessimistic visions of the future and the need to develop alternative narratives.
