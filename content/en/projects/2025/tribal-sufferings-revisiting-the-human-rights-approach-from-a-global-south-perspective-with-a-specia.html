Examines how tribal communities in India have been displaced and marginalised through colonial and neoliberal development models. It questions whether individualistic, market-oriented approaches to rights can adequately address collective injustices. Drawing on Global South critiques of human rights and Nancy Fraser's theory of redistribution and recognition, Mark argues for an approach that protects communal land rights, cultural identities, and collective well-being. The article calls for a more inclusive understanding of development that prioritises communities' rights over purely economic objectives.
