The article examines how civil society groups can organise against democratic backsliding, drawing on cases from Türkiye and Hungary. Yabanci develops a framework for understanding resilient civic opposition, focusing on how movements connect different grievances, combine street protests with other forms of mobilisation, and interact with opposition political parties. The study offers a way to assess the conditions that shape civic resistance to growing authoritarianism beyond formal party politics.
