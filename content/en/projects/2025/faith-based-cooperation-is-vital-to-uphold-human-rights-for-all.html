Religious communities can work together to promote human rights, reduce discrimination and strengthen trust across different beliefs. This OHCHR article introduces the UN's Faith for Rights initiative, which encourages religious leaders and organisations to learn from one another and take practical action. It describes examples of interfaith cooperation, including work in Cyprus, and highlights educational tools for addressing religious intolerance and hate speech.
