Published online 1 September 2025. summary: >-

Examining Türkiye's Specially Authorized Courts, the article explores how governments can exploit authoritarian features inherited from earlier legal systems to weaken democratic institutions. Över argues that the AKP government used these courts to constrain political opponents while presenting institutional reforms and legal proceedings as steps toward democratisation. The study shows how authoritarian legal legacies can serve both to exercise political power and to legitimise democratic backsliding.
