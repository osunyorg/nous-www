Fofana questions approaches to teaching international law that focus on exceptional crises or defending established legal rules. He presents international law as continually shaped by political struggles, competing interpretations and historical change. Drawing on the evolution of self-determination, he argues for greater attention to how legal principles are developed, contested and used for different political purposes.
