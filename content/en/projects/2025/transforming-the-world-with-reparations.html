Brunk, Ingrid, and Monica Hakimi. “Transforming the World with Reparations?” American Journal of International Law 119, no. 3 (2025): 380–396. summary: >-

Brunk and Hakimi distinguish between corrective reparations, which seek to remedy specific legally recognised harms, and transformative reparations, which aim to address wider inequalities rooted in historical injustice. They question whether reparations can realistically achieve such broad structural change and examine the conceptual and legal difficulties of combining these approaches. The article encourages reconsideration of what reparations can accomplish.
