Humby examines TWAIL, environmental justice and decolonisation as distinct but overlapping approaches to international environmental law. Drawing on South Africa's 2015–2016 student movement and debates surrounding Africa's renewable energy transition, she explores how these frameworks can illuminate different dimensions of inequality and power. The article applies them to issues including oil and gas concessions and renewable energy trade disputes.
