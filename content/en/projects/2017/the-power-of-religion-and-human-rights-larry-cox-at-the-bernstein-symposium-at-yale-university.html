Originally delivered as a keynote address on 23 March 2017. summary: >-

Larry Cox examines the relationship between religion, human rights and social movements. He argues that the growing emphasis on legal expertise and professional advocacy has weakened parts of the human rights movement's connection to grassroots struggles. Drawing on civil rights activism and campaigns against poverty, he discusses how religious and secular groups can work together to address inequality, strengthen community leadership and pursue broader social change.
