International Organizations and the Fight for Accountability: The Remedies and Reparations Gap. Oxford University Press, 2017. summary: >-

Ferstman examines why international organisations often fail to provide effective remedies and reparations for violations of human rights and humanitarian law attributed to them. The book identifies legal and institutional barriers to accountability, questions justifications for limiting organisational responsibility, and argues that reparations should reflect the nature of violations and resulting harm rather than the identity of the responsible institution.
