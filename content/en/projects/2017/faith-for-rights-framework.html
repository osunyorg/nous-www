Faith for Rights Framework. 2017–ongoing. summary: >-

The Faith for Rights framework brings together religious leaders, faith-based organisations and human rights actors to promote equality and respect across different beliefs. It encourages cooperation between communities and connects religious teachings with international human rights principles. The initiative provides a shared approach for addressing discrimination, supporting religious freedom and promoting human rights through dialogue and collective action.
