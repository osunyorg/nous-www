Hunt argues that effective human rights implementation requires engagement across the wider UN system, beyond its principal human rights institutions. He distinguishes between the traditional human rights “mainland” and an emerging “archipelago” of initiatives within specialised agencies and other UN bodies. The article proposes greater institutional ownership of human rights and new approaches to cooperation and implementation.
