Palestine Yearbook of International Law, Vol. 15, No. 7, 2010. summary: >-

This article examines how traditional legal education can reinforce unequal power relations and colonial perspectives in international law. Using an international law course as an example, the authors discuss an alternative teaching approach based on critical thinking, student participation and dialogue. They argue that legal education should encourage students to question established legal structures and consider their implications for global justice.
