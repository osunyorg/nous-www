Pineda examines liberation theology as a form of Christian practice rooted in struggles against poverty, oppression and marginalisation. She explains how experiences of injustice and actions taken to challenge it shape theological reflection, rather than the other way around. Although historically associated with Latin America, liberation theology is presented as an approach that can emerge wherever communities seek liberation from oppression.
