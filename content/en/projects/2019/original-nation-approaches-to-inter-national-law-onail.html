Fukurai challenges the state-centred foundations of international law and examines how governments and corporations have contributed to the displacement of Indigenous peoples and environmental destruction. He proposes an alternative approach that places Indigenous nations and their rights at the centre of international relations. The article considers how cooperation between communities, new legal institutions, and approaches recognising nature's rights could support self-determination and protect cultural and environmental diversity.
