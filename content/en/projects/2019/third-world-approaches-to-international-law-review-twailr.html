Third World Approaches to International Law Review (TWAILR). Independent open-access journal and publishing platform, established 2019. summary: >-

The Third World Approaches to International Law Review (TWAILR) provides an open-access platform for scholarship examining international law from Global South perspectives. It publishes research and commentary on colonialism, inequality, resistance and alternative approaches to global governance. By prioritising perspectives often marginalised in legal scholarship, TWAILR seeks to make international legal debates more inclusive and support the development of more equitable legal frameworks.
