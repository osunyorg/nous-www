The podcast explores how colonial histories and structures continue to influence contemporary societies and how people challenge these legacies through activism, scholarship, and cultural practices. Featuring conversations on subjects such as reparations, restitution, and knowledge production, it connects decolonial ideas with practical struggles for change and highlights different approaches to confronting historical and continuing inequalities.
