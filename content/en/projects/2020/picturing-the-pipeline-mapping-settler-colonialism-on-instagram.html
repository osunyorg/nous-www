Through Instagram posts about Canada's Trans Mountain pipeline, Karsgaard and MacDonald examine how images and hashtags shape public debates over development, Indigenous sovereignty, and environmental resistance. Their analysis shows how digital representations connect local experiences to national politics, revealing Instagram as a contested space where settler-colonial assumptions can be reproduced, challenged, and potentially transformed.
