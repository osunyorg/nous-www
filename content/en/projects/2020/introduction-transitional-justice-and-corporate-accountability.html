Payne, Pereira and Bernal-Bermúdez introduce a model of corporate accountability that emphasises the role of civil society and institutional actors in pursuing justice for corporate involvement in human rights violations. They examine obstacles to accountability, including business resistance and limited international pressure, and explain how grassroots demands and institutional innovation can create opportunities for accountability through courts and truth commissions.
