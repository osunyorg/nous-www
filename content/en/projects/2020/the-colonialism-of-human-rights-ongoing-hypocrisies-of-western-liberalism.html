The Colonialism of Human Rights: Ongoing Hypocrisies of Western Liberalism. Polity Press, 2020. summary: >-

Samson challenges claims of human rights universality by examining the historical relationship between Western liberalism, colonialism and slavery. Drawing on examples involving Indigenous peoples and communities affected by racial discrimination, he argues that colonial hierarchies continue to shape whose rights are recognised and protected. The book advocates reparative justice and approaches to law grounded in Indigenous experiences and perspectives.
