McGeoch examines how Liberation Christianity and Liberation Theology have engaged with democracy and human rights in Latin America. He argues that their original emphasis on liberation risks being overshadowed by efforts to defend existing political institutions. Drawing on the idea of institutional imagination, the article considers how liberation movements might develop alternative political projects beyond established democratic and human rights frameworks.
