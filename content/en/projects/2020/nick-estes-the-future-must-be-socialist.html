Nick Estes presents an Indigenous-informed vision of ecological and social justice that places the well-being of the Earth alongside human relationships. He criticises capitalist overconsumption and the displacement of Indigenous peoples from their lands, arguing that environmental protection requires restoring Indigenous stewardship and establishing more equitable social relations. Estes advocates socialism as a necessary alternative for achieving an ecologically sustainable future.
