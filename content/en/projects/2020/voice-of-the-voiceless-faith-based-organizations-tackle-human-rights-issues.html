Faith-based organisations can play an important role in promoting human rights through their connections with local communities. This feature discusses how religious groups address discrimination, religious freedom and hate speech, while identifying challenges such as limited cooperation and legal expertise. It highlights the importance of dialogue across different faiths, human rights education, and translating religious concerns into language that can support wider public advocacy.
