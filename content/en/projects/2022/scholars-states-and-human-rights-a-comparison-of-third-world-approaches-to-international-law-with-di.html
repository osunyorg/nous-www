Europe in a Global Perspective, no. 122 (2022). summary: >-

Weingand compares criticisms of the international human rights system advanced by TWAIL scholars with those expressed by China, Cuba and Egypt during the first three cycles of the UN Universal Periodic Review. He organises TWAIL scholarship around critiques of culture, rhetoric and institutional models, then uses qualitative content analysis to investigate similarities between academic and state-level arguments.
