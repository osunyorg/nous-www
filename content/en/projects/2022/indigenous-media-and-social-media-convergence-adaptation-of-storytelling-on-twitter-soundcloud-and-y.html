Far from disappearing in the digital age, Indigenous storytelling traditions in Zimbabwe are finding new forms of expression on Twitter, SoundCloud, and YouTube. Mpofu shows how oral narratives are adapted, shared, and reinvented through social media, creating opportunities for cultural participation and continuity. These practices challenge assumptions that technological change inevitably erodes traditional knowledge, revealing how digital spaces can sustain and transform Indigenous forms of communication.
