The Responsibility to Protect is often criticised as a Western concept that can justify intervention in other countries. Pison Hindawi challenges this understanding by highlighting how states from the Global South have also influenced its development. She argues that decolonising international law requires recognising their role in shaping international principles and understanding how different countries negotiate competing ideas about universal rights and responsibilities.
