Examining Indigenous-led social media campaigns in Australia, the article explores how Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander communities use digital platforms to challenge colonial representations and assert their own perspectives. The authors describe these interventions as disrupting “colonial algorithms” that shape public understandings of Indigenous identities. It highlights how online activism can make continuing injustices visible and create opportunities for Indigenous communities to influence public narratives.
