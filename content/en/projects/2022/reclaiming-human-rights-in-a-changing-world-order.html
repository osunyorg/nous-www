Reclaiming Human Rights in a Changing World Order. Washington, DC: Brookings Institution Press and Chatham House, 2022. summary: >-

This edited volume examines how geopolitical competition, populism, religious movements, and emerging technologies are challenging international and regional human rights systems. Bringing together scholars and practitioners, it explores threats to established institutions across different regions and proposes ways to strengthen their legitimacy and effectiveness. The book calls for institutional reform, wider participation, and renewed international cooperation, while considering how human rights protections can adapt to a changing global order.
