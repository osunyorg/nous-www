In Earth’s Care [Podcast series]. 2022. summary: >-

This podcast series brings together different voices and perspectives to explore relationships between people, communities, and the Earth. Engaging with Indigenous knowledge, colonial histories, and ecological challenges, it encourages reflection on how dominant approaches to development and sustainability might be reconsidered. The series invites listeners to imagine more responsible and reciprocal ways of relating to one another and the natural world.
