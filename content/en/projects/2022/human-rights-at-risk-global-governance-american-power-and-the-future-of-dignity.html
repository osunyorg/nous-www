Human Rights at Risk: Global Governance, American Power, and the Future of Dignity. Rutgers University Press, 2022. summary: >-

The edited volume brings together interdisciplinary perspectives on the challenges facing the international human rights system, including institutional weaknesses, political contestation, authoritarianism, and inequalities in global governance. Through different case studies, contributors examine gaps between human rights commitments and their implementation, as well as the influence of powerful states on human rights protection. The book also considers possible approaches to strengthening international human rights institutions and safeguarding human dignity.
