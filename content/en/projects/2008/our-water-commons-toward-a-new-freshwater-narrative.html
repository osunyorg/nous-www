Our Water Commons: Toward a New Freshwater Narrative. Council of Canadians / On the Commons, 2008. summary: >-

The report challenges the treatment of freshwater as a commodity to be bought and sold, arguing instead that water should be protected as a shared resource essential to both human communities and nature. It proposes an alternative approach to water governance based on ecological protection, community participation, public responsibility, and the human right to water. Barlow advocates stronger legal protections and greater local control over water resources to promote equitable and sustainable access.
